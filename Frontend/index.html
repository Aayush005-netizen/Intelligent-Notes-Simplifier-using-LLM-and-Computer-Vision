FAQ

Common Questions

What file formats are supported? ▾
NoteAI supports PDF, DOCX, JPG, PNG, WebP, HEIC, and camera captures. Handwritten or printed — we handle both.
How accurate is the OCR? ▾
Our OCR engine achieves ~92% accuracy on clear handwriting and ~98%+ on printed text. Accuracy improves with image quality.
Is my data secure? ▾
Yes. All files are encrypted in transit and at rest. We do not store your raw notes after processing unless you choose to save them.
Can I use it for competitive exam prep? ▾
Absolutely. NoteAI is designed for UPSC, CAT, GATE, JEE, and other exam aspirants. Topic classification and difficulty tagging are specifically built for structured study.
Is there a free plan? ▾
Yes! The free plan allows 10 note uploads per month with basic summarization. Pro plan unlocks unlimited uploads, AI chat, and advanced exports.

✨ AI Summary

Topic Rotational Motion
Level Intermediate
Read ~3 min
Subject Physics

Rotational Motion describes the motion of objects around a fixed axis. The key quantities analogous to linear motion are torque (τ), angular momentum (L), and moment of inertia (I).

Key Points

  • Torque τ = r × F acts as the rotational equivalent of force
  • Newton's 2nd law: τ = Iα (torque = moment of inertia × angular acceleration)
  • Angular momentum L = Iω is conserved when no external torque acts
  • Use the parallel axis theorem (I = I_cm + Md²) to shift the rotation axis
  • Rolling bodies have both translational and rotational kinetic energy

Important Formulas

I_sphere = 2/5 MR²
v = Rω (rolling)
KE = ½Mv² + ½Iω²
